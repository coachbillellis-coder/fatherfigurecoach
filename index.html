Straight Answers

Asked by men like you.

What is the Father Figure Framework?

A faith-grounded coaching system built over five years by a man who lost 90 pounds after age 50. It addresses the whole man — body, mind, and purpose — through metabolic health, habit discipline, and identity work. Educational coaching, not medical treatment.

How do I get rid of the dad bod after 40?

Not with crash diets. Sustainable change comes from protein-anchored nutrition, strength training to defend your muscle, stable blood sugar, real sleep, and accountability. The dad bod is a habit pattern — and habits can be rebuilt. This is educational information, not medical advice.

I'm on a GLP-1 medication. Can coaching still help me?

Yes. GLP-1s reduce appetite, but they don't build the eating, strength, and habit patterns that protect muscle and keep results after the prescription ends. Coaching alongside your physician's plan helps make the change permanent.

What does faith have to do with fitness?

Scripture calls the body a temple and health a stewardship. For many men, discipline finally sticks when it's anchored to something bigger than a mirror — leading their family well, for the long haul.
